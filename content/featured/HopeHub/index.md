Built a **full-stack CRM platform** for nonprofits to manage client cases and generate grant reports, replacing manual workflows with real-time, cloud-based automation. Integrated the **Gemini API** for AI-powered report generation and used **Firebase Firestore** to enable dynamic tracking of client services and outcomes. **Adopted by Fourth & Hope** (Nonprofit based in Sacramento, CA), where the platform now supports daily operations in case management and grant reporting, demonstrating lasting social and technical impact.
