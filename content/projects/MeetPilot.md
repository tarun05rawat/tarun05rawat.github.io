A real-time meeting assistant desktop app built with React, Electron, and Node.js, powered by FastAPI, Firebase, and GPT-4. Captures background audio with live transcription via Deepgram while an agentic GPT-4 workflow auto-generates summaries, action items, sentiment analysis, and decision tracking.
